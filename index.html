About

About PurpleStar SOS

PurpleStar SOS is an interactive learning platform that brings symbolic languages together so their patterns, relationships, and synchronicities can be explored in one place.

Its purpose is simple: Making the Mystical Practical.

Astrology. Numerology. Nomenology. Tarot. Psychology. Mythology. Nature. Color. The Human Body.

Each system offers a different perspective. Together, they create a living network of interconnected symbols that can be explored one connection at a time.

Rather than studying each symbolic language separately, PurpleStar SOS reveals where they intersect, overlap, and reinforce one another. Every connection becomes another way to explore the larger symbolic picture.

These symbols aren't just concepts to memorize. They become tools for self-discovery, reflection, growth, and ultimately the creation of the most meaningful character you'll ever build... Yourself!

Why I Made This

It’s my brain... organized.

Years of books. Thousands of pages of notes. Software projects. Conversations. Readings. Questions. Research. A lifelong curiosity about the many ways humans have searched for meaning.

This is not just some random AI-generated cyber-gunk. It is a compiled database of books, websites, infographics, personal notes, and symbolic research that I have organized into a system that allows me to break inside my own mainframe.

Over time, the boundaries between subjects disappeared. A tarot card connects to a planet. A planet connects to a zodiac sign. A zodiac sign echoes through mythology. A number reveals an archetype. A name carries language, history, and symbolism.

The vision of a wheel within a wheel offers a fitting image. Each wheel represents a symbolic language. Each turns independently while remaining connected to every other wheel.

About Me

I have been obsessed with astrology and web design for as long as I can remember.

I wrote my first BASIC program when I was around six or seven years old, and I started looking at astrology when I was around nine.

Years of MySpace coding led to me owning my own domain while I was still in junior high. From there came coding bootcamps, working in tech, and continuing to build websites and software projects.

Building PurpleStar SOS is reminiscent of the MySpace days. It gives me a place to code, experiment, organize information, and make the kind of website I want to explore.

Why PurpleStar?

Stars emit an entire spectrum of light, yet the human eye does not perceive stars as truly green or truly purple. That scientific curiosity became a symbolic reminder that some of the most meaningful aspects of life are not immediately visible.

Green represents the heart, growth, healing, and relationship. Purple represents intuition, imagination, wisdom, and mystery.

PurpleStar invites us to look beyond the obvious and explore the unseen connections woven throughout our lives.

Every module is another lens. Every connection offers another perspective. Every perspective contributes to a larger understanding.

🔒 Privacy & Local Data

YOUR SYMBOLIC WORK BELONGS TO YOU.

Privacy First

Everything you enter, including notes, reflections, saved analyses, Star Traveler profiles, journal entries, personal observations, and settings, is stored locally on your device through your browser.

PurpleStar SOS does not upload this information to a PurpleStar SOS server or save it in an internal cloud database.

Symbolic Processing

PurpleStar SOS performs symbolic calculations through the correspondences, interpretation framework, and synthesis system built specifically for this application. Your personal entries are not processed through third-party artificial intelligence systems during normal application use.

Public Online Sources

Two features retrieve requested information from free, publicly available online web sources:

1 // NAME SEARCH

Name meanings, history, and related references are retrieved from public online web sources.

2 // ZODIAC NATAL CHARTS

Astronomical and natal chart calculation information is retrieved from public free online web sources to generate the requested chart.

These online lookups are separate from your locally stored notes, saved analyses, and Star Traveler records.

A Private Symbolic Browser

Using PurpleStar SOS is similar to using a private browser. Instead of creating accounts across many websites and scattering information between multiple tabs, PurpleStar SOS brings symbolic tools into one organized workspace and stores your saved work locally on your own device.

PurpleStar SOS was built to organize symbolic information, not collect personal information.
